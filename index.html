Why HealthONE

A neutral convening and execution platform

Healthcare innovation often stalls between a promising idea and real-world adoption. HealthONE brings the relevant stakeholders into one coordinated process, aligns incentives, defines measurable outcomes and supports execution across organizational boundaries.

Our role is not to replace existing innovation units. It is to connect them, strengthen their reach and help turn fragmented capabilities into shared national and international value.

Initial regional focus

Strengthening the healthcare ecosystem in Northern Israel

HealthONE's first major area of activity is the healthcare system in Northern Israel—a diverse region with strong medical, academic and technological capabilities and significant opportunities to improve access, continuity, resilience and regional collaboration.

Our regional ambition

To help build a connected, resilient and internationally engaged healthcare ecosystem in the North over the coming years.

PatientsMore equitable access, including care closer to home and stronger continuity between community and hospital care.
Clinical teamsOpportunities to lead innovation, collaborate across organizations and remain professionally connected to the region.
ResearchersMulti-institution research, shared capabilities and stronger pathways for advanced data-driven work.
Regional growthA stronger environment for health ventures, investment, talent and long-term economic resilience.
